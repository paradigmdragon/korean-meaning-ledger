# Commercial Licensing

Korean Meaning Ledger is intended to be freely accessible for personal, educational, research, and noncommercial use while sustaining development through commercial licensing.

## Commercial use includes

Examples include, but are not limited to:

- embedding the Runtime in a paid product, app, SaaS, API, or hosted service;
- using the Runtime or Meaning dataset in internal commercial operations;
- customer delivery, consulting deliverables, or enterprise deployment;
- commercial model training, evaluation, or decision systems that materially depend on this project;
- redistributing the Meaning dataset or a materially derived commercial dataset;
- reselling hosted access to this project's Runtime or canonical Meaning data.

## Commercial terms

Commercial terms are not yet standardized.

Early commercial users should obtain a separate written license from the project owner before deployment.

Possible future models include:

- annual commercial license;
- usage-based hosted/API access;
- enterprise deployment and support;
- commercial dataset license;
- OEM / redistribution agreement.

## What this does not change

Commercial licensing does not automatically transfer ownership of the canonical Meaning Ledger, trademarks, or project governance.
